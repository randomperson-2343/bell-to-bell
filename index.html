How to Play

The goal

Each trading day runs from the opening bell (9:30) to the closing bell (4:00), which takes about four real minutes. Make as much money as you can. Your boss sets a daily quota. Miss it too often and you're done.

Trading

  • Buy goes long and makes money when a stock rises. Sell closes a long position, or goes short when you're flat and makes money when it falls.
  • Leverage: you can hold up to 4x your equity during the day, but only 2x overnight. Anything over that is force-sold at the close.
  • Margin call: if your equity falls too far, an alarm sounds and a countdown starts. Cut positions or risk will dump your worst ones for you.
  • Limit and stop orders wait for a price. SL% and TP% attach an automatic stop-loss and take-profit to a market order.
  • Options: buy calls (bets that it goes up) or puts (bets that it goes down). You pay the premium in full, the payoff can be huge, and they expire.
  • Watch the Wire for real news. The Chirp feed is rumors. Some are real and early, many are fake.
  • FEAR tracks market panic. When it's high, spreads widen, options get expensive and slippage bites.

Stress

Losses, leverage, margin calls, ringing phones and a looming close all push up your stress. High stress shakes the screen and makes your hands slip (fat-finger orders). At 100 you have a panic attack and can't trade for a while. Going flat or taking a coffee break brings it down.
